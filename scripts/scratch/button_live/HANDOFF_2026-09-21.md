## What works (proven on hardware today)

**Two full autonomous presses of the top-right (`timer_clock`) button from a ~17 cm standoff**,
via `scripts/scratch/button_live/press_button_autonomous.py`: pixel servo (button pixel onto
the fixed left-fingertip pixel, 74.8 px → 1.4 px in two moves) → 2 mm steps along the
fingertip ray → contact by a sharp force jump (+5.2 N and +2.25 N in one step, at 1.6 cm) →
1 mm press → 2 cm retract → back to standoff. Tracking 0.1–0.4 mm per step, joints converge to
0.01°. The microwave registered both presses (display went to clock-set `0:00`).

Command that did it (from the 17 cm standoff, arm already looking at the panel):
```bash
P=scripts/scratch/button_live/press_button_autonomous.py
python3 $P --target timer_clock --execute --tip-dist 0.154 --presses 1 --fine-step 0.002 --press-travel 0.001 --cap-override 0.03
```
`--tip-dist 0.154` is MEASURED (plane 17.0 cm along the ray, contact at 1.6 cm), not a ruler
guess — the ruler numbers given during the session (6 cm / 5.5 cm) were wrong by ~10 cm.

Perception that feeds it, also proven: `button_detector_node.py` (5 reference views in
`~/wrist_ref_red/`, named buttons `start_30s` + `timer_clock`, `-p target_button:=…`) and
`detect_button_press_force.py --publish` (tool wrench → `/press_detector/*`, overlay banner).

## What does NOT work yet: starting from far away (~32 cm)

The claw pixel is near the bottom of the frame, so aligning the button onto it from 32 cm drags
the small panel behind the finger → detector abstains ("quad not convex"). A **two-phase**
approach was added (far phase holds the button 110 px above the claw and advances along that
ray until the panel is 20 cm away, then the proven close phase). Its far phase ran twice and
stopped both times, for two different reasons, both now addressed in code but **the fixed
version has not been run**:

1. 2 cm far steps swung joints 7–10° and Kinova's external-wrench *estimate* shifted 3–5 N
   with no contact (phantom force). Fix: far phase aborts only on ≥15 N (no contact is
   geometrically possible there), and the press detector is re-baselined by `SIGUSR1` before
   the close phase and before the approach (`Run.rebaseline_force`).
2. **Redundancy drift**: re-seeding IK from the current joints each step let J1/J3
   counter-rotate more every step (−7 → −10.6° and +6.7 → +9.9° for identical 2 cm moves,
   −30°/+26° total after 6 cm) until the 10° joint-jump gate refused. This is also what made
   (1) worse. Fix: every IK is now seeded from the run's **start posture** (`Arm.solve_translation(
   posture=…)`, fallback to current-seed). Untested on hardware.

Also untested: `MIN_INLIERS` 12 (start gate) / `MIN_INLIERS_TRACK` 8 (transit re-servo skips
instead of aborting on a weak lock) — the lock dips to ~13–15 between reference views 5 (32 cm)
and 3 (~27 cm).

## Gotchas learned today (each cost real time)

- **`arm_server`'s Kortex session dies when new Kortex sessions are opened** (three times,
  each right after restarting the force detector). Symptom: `INVALID_USER_SESSION_ACCESS` on
  every `get_state()`, `/joint_states` goes silent. Also dies after ~10 s with NO client traffic
  (`session_inactivity_timeout=10000` in `kinova.py`, no keepalive) — start
  `joint_state_bridge` within 10 s of `arm_server`; it is the keepalive. Recovery order:
  `arm_server` → bridge → stub base → bypass → `arm_set_speed.py low`. Never restart the force
  detector while the arm stack is up — use `kill -USR1 <pid>` to re-baseline it.
- **Force detector baseline goes stale after any move** (pose-dependent wrench bias, and the
  detector stops adapting above 4 N). `SIGUSR1` re-baselines live. The close-phase contact rule
  is now *rise ≥ 2.5 N since approach start AND ≥ 1.5 N jump within one step* — drift showed
  jumps up to 1.42 N with 2 mm steps, real contact 2.25–5.2 N, so the margin is thin; slack the
  wrist camera cable (suspected contributor) and consider `JUMP_N` 2.0 with 1.5 mm steps.
- **`add_reference_view.py` placed a named button on label text** (6–8-inlier projection landed
  one dome pitch off; the fit "found" a 1.4 px blob). Now refuses projections under 15 inliers
  and fits whose radius differs >30% from the primary dome; pass `--also NAME X Y` from the
  overlay. Always eyeball the new view's marks (`scratchpad/view4_marks.png` pattern).
- **Stale pixels**: the node runs at ~5 Hz; after a move only accept a `button_pixel` received
  after the arm settled (`Perception.wait_after`), or the servo removes ~50 % per iteration.
- Depth at 17 cm is at the D435i's minimum range but was *correct* today (contact landed where
  the plane said); the ruler was the thing that lied.
- `pkill -f <script name>` from a Claude shell kills the shell itself (the pattern matches the
  wrapper). Use `pgrep -f "^python3 -u <path>"` + `kill <pid>`.
